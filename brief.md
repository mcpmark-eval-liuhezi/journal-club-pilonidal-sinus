## 1. Centerpiece Paper

**Citation:** Tokocin, Yigitbas, Yavuz, Gulcicek. *Surgical Treatment Outcomes for Primary Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Single-Centre Retrospective Study of 2222 Patients (2006-2020).* **International Wound Journal**, 2026. **PMID: 42633928.**

- **Journal:** International Wound Journal
- **Publication year:** 2026
- **Authors:** Tokocin, Yigitbas, Yavuz, Gulcicek
- **PMID:** 42633928
- **Design:** Single-centre retrospective cohort, Turkish tertiary referral centre (surgery Jan 2006 – Dec 2020)
- **Full-text availability:** PMC article page https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13500213/ (automated PDF retrieval blocked — see notes below)

### Key Numbers for the Club

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Cohort size | **2222** patients with primary pilonidal sinus disease |
| Minimum follow-up | **5 years** |
| Surgical techniques compared | **Nine** techniques (lay-open, Karydakis flap, Bascom cleft lift, Limberg flap, Dufourmentel flap, among others) |
| 5-year cumulative recurrence | **7.9%** (175/2222) |
| Overall recurrence | **10.2%** (226/2222) |
| Postoperative infection | **6.9%** (154/2222) |
| Difference between techniques (recurrence & infection) | both **p < 0.001** |
| Median time to recurrence | **20 months** (IQR 12–36); most within first 2 years |

**Lowest recurrence rates by technique:** Karydakis flap **1.5%**, Bascom cleft lift **2.3%**, Dufourmentel flap **2.4%**.

**Adjusted recurrence risk vs lay-open (Cox regression):**
- Karydakis flap: HR 0.19 (95% CI 0.12–0.30)
- Bascom cleft lift: HR 0.25 (95% CI 0.14–0.43)
- Dufourmentel flap: HR 0.26 (95% CI 0.12–0.57)
- Limberg flap: HR 0.32 (95% CI 0.19–0.53)
- Higher BMI independently increased recurrence risk (HR 1.05 per 1 kg/m²)

**Methods:** Kaplan–Meier estimates and multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression; recurrence analyzed as time-to-event. Patients with recurrent disease at presentation were excluded.
